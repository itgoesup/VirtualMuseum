Stories of Derinkuyu!


1. How Derinkuyu was found

Now as mentioned on the front page, the way Derinkuyu was found was when a man was renovating his basement. But the more detailed version starts in 1963 in Turkey.

This man was knocking down his wall, and found a hidden room. When he was checking out the hidden room, he discovered an entry and stumbled into Derinkuyu.

2. The great seige survival

Around the Arab and Byzantine wars, Derinkuyu was a refuge for Christians. The way that the families would have to live was in the dark and silence because of how the doors blocked everything off, but it was safer as it could only be opened from the inside.

3. Spirits in the walls

Before the offical discovery of Derinkuyu, the locals would tell stories about spirits living in their basement. What was mainly heard was muffled voices or the sounds of footsteps. But in reality, it was more than likely animals falling into the ventalation shafts.

4. Spirits of Djinn

Still on the topic of spirits, back around in the 1920's when Derinkuyu was abandoned, the locals avoided deep caves. The people believed that whoever went deep into the caves without a source of light would be led by the spirits and be stuck in the labyrinth forever.
